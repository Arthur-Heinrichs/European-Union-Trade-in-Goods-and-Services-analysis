Notes:

Codes such as EA21 and EA12 (euro area aggregates) are not included in the mapping table.
Although their meaning could be inferred, they were intentionally left unmapped to avoid assumptions not explicitly defined by Eurostat.
2. Variable: unit

Defines how the economic values are measured.

Possible values:
CP_MEUR → Current prices, million euro
CP_MNAC → Current prices, million units of national currency
CLV20_MEUR → Chain linked volumes (2020), million euro
CLV15_MEUR → Chain linked volumes (2015), million euro
CLV10_MEUR → Chain linked volumes (2010), million euro
Methodological notes:
CP_MNAC (national currency series):
Reported by Member States and correspond to nationally published figures. These values include:
volume component
price (inflation) component
exchange rate effects (when the currency is not the euro)
CP_MEUR (euro series):
Derived from national currency series using historical exchange rates.
Suitable for internal comparison and aggregation
When analyzing over time, exchange rate effects must be considered
For euro area Member States:
National currency series are converted using irrevocably fixed exchange rates
This preserves growth rates from the original national series
Values match after euro adoption but differ in earlier years due to market exchange rate fluctuations
3. Variable: na_item

Represents the type of national accounts transaction related to trade flows.

Classification:
P.6 → Exports of goods and services
P.7 → Imports of goods and services

4. General Notes
The dataset is based on Eurostat national accounts data.
Trade variables follow ESA (European System of Accounts) standards.
Values may differ depending on whether they are expressed in national currency or euros, especially for historical series.
